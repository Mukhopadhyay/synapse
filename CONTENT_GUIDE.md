# Content guide

How Synapse notes read. Short is a feature.

- **One idea per note.** If you need a second heading for a different concept, it's probably a second note.
- **Summary: one sentence** a newcomer can follow. It shows on hover, in search and under the title.
- **Plain, short, sentence case.** "Self-attention", not "Self-Attention". No emoji.
- **Explain, then show.** A few short paragraphs, then one example, formula or code snippet.
- **Only relations you can say out loud.** "Attention requires Dot product." If the sentence sounds wrong, pick another relation or leave it out.
  - `requires`: you need this first.
  - `extends`: this is a kind of that.
  - `related`: worth reading together.
  - `contrasts`: an alternative to.
- **Link generously in the body** with `[[slug]]`; links to notes that don't exist yet are fine and show up as ghosts.
- **Cite what you lean on** under `resources` (title and url).
- **Show a key source as a card**: put its URL alone on a line (`!` in front for a large card with its image), at most one or two per note.
